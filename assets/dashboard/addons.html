    Third-party libraries
    
Anyone can keep a library of add-ons of their own, laid out as the catalogue is. Name one here and what is in it is listed above, beside the catalogue's.

    
Nobody at HomeCanvas has tested or read what is in another library. An add-on written in Dart is code: once installed it runs on the panel as the panel does, and can read whatever the panel can — its settings and keys among them. What a library says its add-ons use is its own word. Add only a library whose owner you trust; what you install from one is at your own risk.

    
Add-ons written in Dart from another library are installed only while "Widgets written in Dart" is switched on in Settings → Dashboard, on the panel itself. Those described in JSON need no switch.
