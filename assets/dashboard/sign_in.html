Sign in from this browser

  1. Add the Get cookies.txt LOCALLY extension from your browser's add-on store (Chrome, Edge, Brave or Firefox). Not the older "Get cookies.txt" without "LOCALLY" — it was withdrawn for sending people's cookies away.
  2. Open a private / incognito window and allow the extension to run there (the browser asks, or it is in the extension's settings).
  3. In that window, go to youtube.com and sign in — Premium, your subscriptions, whatever the panel should have.
  4. Still on youtube.com, open the extension and choose Export (as Netscape format) to save a cookies.txt file.
  5. Close the private window without signing out. Signing out would cancel the cookies you just saved; closing the window leaves the panel as their only user.
  6. Choose that file below, or paste what is in it.
